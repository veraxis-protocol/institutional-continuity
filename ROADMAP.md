# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and non-normative.

## Formation

- stabilize category constitution and canonical vocabulary;
- establish exact byte authority and release discipline;
- harden ICTS aggregation semantics;
- preserve adversarial findings and successor lineage.

## Falsification

- publish bounded conformance methodology;
- expand semantic, authority, consequence, observation, reconciliation, and examination vectors;
- evaluate Veraxis under the same rules as external systems;
- obtain genuinely independent reproductions.

## Interoperability

- freeze compatibility-profile schema;
- publish handoff contracts;
- enable third-party implementations to declare J1–J8 coverage without adopting Veraxis internals.

## Formalization

- formalize core non-collapse and witness-path properties;
- publish kernel-checked claims only with exact toolchain and artifact identities.

## Ecosystem

- publish neutral landscape mappings;
- invite external profiles and evaluations;
- develop standards submissions for appropriate protocol-sized pieces rather than forcing the entire category into one protocol.

## Long-term

The success criterion is not that every system uses Veraxis software. It is that consequential agent/automation infrastructure can state precisely which institutional-continuity joins it establishes, under what evidence, and how an independent examiner can verify that claim.
