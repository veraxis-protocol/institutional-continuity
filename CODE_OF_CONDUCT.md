# Community Code of Conduct

ICI is intended to support rigorous disagreement about high-consequence infrastructure.

Participants are expected to:

- criticize claims, evidence, assumptions, and designs rather than people;
- distinguish observation from inference;
- disclose material conflicts of interest in evaluations and reviews;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks;
- respect confidentiality and responsible security disclosure;
- correct material errors when discovered;
- preserve failed evidence rather than rewriting history;
- avoid knowingly presenting bounded results as universal conclusions.

Maintainers may moderate or remove conduct that materially disrupts technical collaboration or creates safety/security risk.

Good-faith technical dissent is not a conduct violation merely because it is uncomfortable or adverse to a project claim.
