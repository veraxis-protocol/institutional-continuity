# Authority Planes

ICI separates authority that is commonly collapsed in automation systems.

## Institutional authority

Authority to determine, admit, approve, or supersede institutional meaning and control.

Questions include:

- who has standing?
- for what scope?
- under which source/version?
- for what period?
- with what delegation or approval boundary?

## Causal authority

Authority or practical capability to make the action or consequence happen.

This includes runtime mediators, credentials, recovery paths, override paths, update authority, alternate invocation channels, and other causal control.

## Evidentiary authority

Authority/control over the record that will later be used to establish what happened.

A system in which the same actor can both cause a consequential event and exclusively manufacture the authoritative account of that event has an evidentiary concentration risk.

## Why separation matters

A valid institutional control does not prove the runtime could not be bypassed.

A valid runtime receipt does not prove consequence completeness.

A causal actor's self-report may be useful evidence without being sufficient evidence for population completeness.

ICI profiles should declare where these planes are controlled and where independent observation/reconciliation enters.
