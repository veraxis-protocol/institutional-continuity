# Core Institutional Continuity Invariants

**Status:** PRE-1.0 normative design baseline

## ICI-F1 — No unadmitted semantic widening

A downstream representation must not broaden a material actor, action, object, modality, condition, exception, threshold, evidence obligation, temporal bound, jurisdiction/scope, priority, remedy, uncertainty state, or consequence class without the required new admission.

## ICI-F2 — No authority inflation

No downstream stage may acquire institutional authority merely because an upstream object was present, signed, logically warranted, or technically accepted.

## ICI-F3 — Unknown non-collapse

Unknown, unresolved, indeterminate, not-established, and not-evaluated states must not silently become established fact.

## ICI-F4 — Exact-action binding

Runtime authority must bind the material action actually executed, not merely an action family or mutable reference.

## ICI-F5 — Consequence evidence cannot self-certify completeness

Evidence produced by the causal path may establish an event record but cannot, by itself, establish that the event population is complete.

## ICI-F6 — Correction preserves history

Correction produces a successor relationship and must not rewrite relied-upon historical bytes or conclusions as though the predecessor never existed.

## ICI-F7 — Examination availability

Historical examination must remain possible under the declared availability model. Profiles requiring offline examination must not depend on live vendor availability.

## ICI-F8 — Unbroken witness path

Every consequence claimed under full ICI has a verifiable witness path across every required canonical join to admitted institutional meaning.

## ICI-F9 — Causal/evidentiary separation

The ability to cause an action must not be silently equated with exclusive authority to establish the authoritative evidence of that action or consequence.

## ICI-F10 — No silent success on error

Malformed input, parser failure, unresolved profile state, integrity failure, or internal evaluation error cannot become a success disposition.

## ICI-F11 — Mode-equivalence discipline

Shadow, replay, preflight, and enforcement modes must not silently use materially different semantic paths when equivalence is claimed.

## ICI-F12 — Change invalidation

Material changes to source, admission, control, authority, profile, schema semantics, evaluator semantics, or required dependencies invalidate affected eligibility until the required successor evidence is established.

## Full witness target

For every eligible consequence `c` claimed under full Institutional Continuity, there exists an independently verifiable witness path through all required nodes/joins, with no semantic widening, authority inflation, unknown collapse, unwitnessed material transition, historical erasure, or self-certified population completeness.
