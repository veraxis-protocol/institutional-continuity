# Canonical Chain

> **SOURCE → ADMITTED MEANING → WARRANTED CONTROL → RUNTIME AUTHORITY → EXACT ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION → RECONCILIATION → EXAMINATION**

The chain is a category-level ontology.

## Nodes

### Source

Authoritative source material or authoritative source state.

### Admitted Meaning

Meaning accepted through the institutionally valid admission process. Extraction, model output, or analyst interpretation is not admission by itself.

### Warranted Control

A machine-operational control connected to admitted meaning by declared warrant, evidence, scope, currentness, and uncertainty treatment.

### Runtime Authority

Current authority eligible to govern the exact runtime decision.

### Exact Action

The consequential action including all material parameters relevant to authorization and consequence.

### Consequence

The externally relevant effect, not merely an executor's assertion that an action was attempted.

### Independent Observation

Observation whose evidentiary authority is not controlled solely by the same mechanism that caused the consequence.

### Reconciliation

Comparison of continuity records to the relevant independently observed population.

### Examination

Later reconstruction, verification, and challenge of the chain under the declared availability model.

## Product-specific substructure

Implementations may introduce delivery artifacts, envelopes, receipts, manifests, signatures, adapters, caches, or other internal stages.

Those stages live **inside** canonical joins unless a future category-version change explicitly modifies the canonical ontology.
