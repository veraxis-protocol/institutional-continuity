# Patent Notice and IPR Boundary

## Purpose

ICI is intended to be broadly implementable as open infrastructure while keeping copyright, patent, trademark, standards, and implementation rights distinct.

This file prevents one common category error: **open documentation does not mean every underlying implementation method is patent-licensed.**

## Documentation license

Specifications and documentation marked `CC-BY-4.0` are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

That license expressly distinguishes patent and trademark rights from the copyright and similar rights it grants. Publication of a specification under CC BY 4.0 therefore must not be interpreted as an additional patent license.

## Apache-2.0 software

Files marked `Apache-2.0` are governed by the Apache License, Version 2.0, including its patent provisions.

Any patent grant exists only to the extent stated by that license and only for material actually licensed under Apache-2.0.

Do not infer an Apache patent grant for:

- CC-BY-4.0 specifications;
- unlicensed or proprietary implementations;
- methods not contributed to the Apache-2.0 work;
- external Veraxis products merely referenced by this repository.

## Existing and future patent rights

Veraxis Protocol and contributors may own, pursue, license, or later disclose patent rights relevant to parts of the broader technical landscape.

The absence of a patent number or disclosure in this repository is **not** a representation that no relevant patent rights exist.

Likewise, mentioning a patent or application does not establish validity, scope, enforceability, infringement, or freedom to operate.

## Open interoperability boundary

The project's intended open surface includes category vocabulary, handoff identities, interoperability schemas, verifier behavior, failure semantics, public benchmark methodology, conformance vectors selected for publication, and compatibility profiles.

The project does not promise that all implementation methods used to produce or discover those objects are open or patent-free.

## Standards submissions

If material from this repository is later submitted to an external standards body, the applicable standards organization's IPR policy may create additional disclosure or licensing duties. Those duties are governed by the relevant submission/process, not by implication from this repository.

## No freedom-to-operate opinion

Nothing in this repository is a patent clearance, non-infringement opinion, or freedom-to-operate analysis.

For formal rights inquiries, use the rights/contact channel published by Veraxis Protocol at `veraxis.io`.
