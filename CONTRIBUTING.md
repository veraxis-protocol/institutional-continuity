# Contributing

Contributions are welcome from implementers, researchers, auditors, formal-methods practitioners, standards participants, regulated institutions, and competing vendors.

## Contribution principle

A good contribution makes the system:

- more precise;
- more falsifiable;
- more interoperable;
- less dependent on implicit trust;
- clearer about evidence and claim ceilings.

## Before proposing a normative change

Read:

1. `spec/ICI-ARCHITECTURE-CONSTITUTION-v0.2.md` (current; supersedes
   `spec/CATEGORY-CONSTITUTION.md`, which is preserved as historical normative evidence)
2. `CLAIMS.md`
3. `GOVERNANCE.md`
4. `spec/CHANGE-CONTROL.md`

Normative changes must include compatibility and benchmark impact, not only prose edits.

## DCO sign-off

All commits intended for contribution should include a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off:

`Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email@example.com>`

By signing off, you certify the contribution under the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1. See `DCO.txt`.

## Licensing of contributions

Accepted contributions are licensed under the license designated for the target file/path unless a separate written agreement applies.

Do not contribute material you do not have the right to license.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted work is permitted, but the human or accountable submitting contributor remains responsible for:

- rights to submit the material;
- factual and technical accuracy;
- provenance of copied material;
- test results;
- security consequences;
- ensuring generated text/code does not silently introduce incompatible licensing.

For material AI assistance, disclose it in the pull-request description when it materially affected the contribution.

AI use does not increase an evidence class. An AI-assisted review is not “independent reproduction” merely because a different model produced it.

## No historical rewriting

Do not “clean up” frozen evidence by altering old results. Submit a successor record.

## Pull-request expectations

A substantive PR should state:

- **Problem**
- **Proposed change**
- **Normative impact**
- **Compatibility impact**
- **Benchmark impact**
- **Security/abuse impact**
- **Evidence**
- **Claim ceiling**
- **Migration/supersession**, if any

## Evaluation contributions

An evaluation must identify exact:

- system/version;
- criteria/profile;
- vector set;
- environment;
- runner/toolchain;
- result;
- evidence references;
- known limitations.

No vendor, including Veraxis, receives privileged credit.

## Conduct

Participation is governed by `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
